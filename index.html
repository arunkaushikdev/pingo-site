What it does

Built around how people really forget

🔔

Alerts you can feel

Low, medium or high priority. High rings and keeps nagging until you answer. Choose ring, vibration and sound for each task.

🪟

Pops up over anything

The reminder appears over your other apps and on the lock screen, with Snooze, Done and Skip right there.

⏱️

Every few hours

"Water every 2 hours between 9 and 9" or fixed times like 9 AM and 9 PM for medicine. One task, many reminders.

🎤

Say it, done

Tap the mic and say "kal shaam 6 baje dawai" or "urgent submit report in 30 minutes". English and Hinglish both work.

🔥

Streaks and progress

Tap Taken or +1 glass from the notification and watch your streak grow for water, medicine and meals.

🎯

Focus and quiet hours

Pomodoro and Focus mode silence reminders for a while. Quiet hours do it every night, and high priority can still get through.

⏰

Several heads-ups

Remind me a day before, an hour before, and again at the time. Templates start you off in one tap.

🏠

Widget

See what is next on your home screen, and add a reminder with one tap.

🔒

Offline and private

No account, no sign-up, works without internet. Your reminders stay on your phone, and you can back them up to a file.

Good to know

Some phones hide reminders to save battery

Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Oppo, Vivo, Realme and Huawei phones can block pop-ups and late reminders unless you allow a few settings. Pingo has a Permission check in Settings that walks you through them. If a reminder is late or missing, that is the first place to look, and we want to hear about it either way.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The test is free and nothing is charged. Ads and paid features are not part of this test.

What do I need?

An Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer, the Google Play Store, and a Gmail or Google account. iPhone is not supported yet.

Why do you need my Gmail?

Play lets only the Google accounts on the tester list join a closed test. We use the address only to add you to that list and to contact you about the test.

How much time will it take?

Five minutes to set up, then use it as a normal reminder app. We would love a short note when something is confusing or broken, but there are no required reports.

What happens to my reminders?

They stay on your phone. Pingo has no account and works offline. You can export a backup file yourself whenever you like. See the privacy page.

Can I leave the test?

Yes, any time. We only ask that you stay for the full 14 days if you can, because the count restarts for everyone when someone leaves early.

Help us make reminders that work

Twelve volunteers, two weeks, a few minutes. Thank you for being one of the first.
