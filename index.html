Why spend two weeks on a new app?

Fair question. Here is the honest trade: a little of your time for a real say in the app, and your name on something you helped build.

What we ask

  • 5 minutes to set up
  • A few minutes a day for 14 days, just by using your reminders
  • Honest feedback when something is off

You can leave whenever you like. We only ask you to stay the full 14 days if you can.

What you get

  • A real say. Every bug report and idea is read by the person who wrote the code, and the best ones ship.
  • Your name on the Wall of Thanks, with your role, whether you are a student or a working professional.
  • A Founding Tester certificate with its own verification link, ready to add to LinkedIn or a CV as proof of hands-on testing work.
  • Early access to every feature before the public release.
  • A tool that fixes a real problem: missed meals, medicine and deadlines.

Who is behind Pingo. Arun Datt Kaushik, an independent Android developer who also makes PDF Toolkit on Google Play. Pingo has no company behind it and no account to create, so there is nothing to sell you.

Join in four steps

Google requires every new app to be tested by at least 12 real people, who stay opted in for 14 days in a row, before it can go public. That is why we need you.

  1. Join the tester group

    Use the Gmail you have on your Android phone. Joining the group is what gives that account access to the test version. It takes one tap and needs no approval from us.

    Join the tester group

  2. Tell us who you are

    A short form: your name, whether you are a student or a professional, and where you study or work. This is only for the Wall of Thanks and your certificate, and your name is shown only if you tick the box.

    Fill the 1-minute form

  3. Accept the invite and install

    Open the test link on your Android phone, tap Become a tester, then install Pingo from Play. It is a normal Play Store install.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The test is free and nothing is charged. Ads and paid features are not part of this test.

What do I need?

An Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer, the Google Play Store, and a Gmail or Google account. iPhone is not supported yet.

Why do you need my Gmail?

Play lets only the Google accounts on a tester list join a closed test, so the Gmail on your phone is how it knows you. We use it only for that and to contact you about the test.

Will my name be shown?

Only if you tick the box in the form. If you do, your name, role and organisation appear on the Wall of Thanks. If you do not, you still count as a tester, and nothing about you is shown. You can ask us to remove your name at any time.

How do I get my certificate?

When the 14 days are done and the app is live, each person on the Wall of Thanks gets a Founding Tester certificate page with a link that anyone can open to verify it. You can download it as an image or add it to LinkedIn from there.

How much time will it take?

About 5 minutes to set up, then a few minutes a day, just from using your reminders. The 14 days run on their own in the background.

I do not have a Gmail account. Can I still join?

Play needs a Google account to give you access, but you can create a free one in a minute and use it on your phone only for this.

Why do some phones need extra settings?

Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Oppo, Vivo, Realme and Huawei phones can block pop-ups and late reminders to save battery. Pingo has a Permission check in Settings that walks you through them. If a reminder is late or missing, tell us, because that is exactly what we are testing.

What happens to my reminders?

They stay on your phone. Pingo has no account and works offline. You can export a backup file yourself whenever you like. See the privacy page.

Can I leave the test?

Yes, any time. We only ask that you stay for the full 14 days if you can, because the count restarts for everyone when someone leaves early.
