Privacy for testers

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for the closed test. This page describes the test version. A full privacy policy will replace it before Pingo is released to everyone.

What stays on your phone

Pingo has no account and no server of its own. Your reminders, history, streaks and settings are stored only on your phone, and the app works without internet.

What the app needs and why

  • Notifications, exact alarms, display over other apps, full-screen alerts: to ring on time and show the reminder pop-up.
  • Battery and autostart settings: so the system does not stop reminders.
  • Microphone (speech recognition): only when you tap the mic to add a reminder by voice. Your phone's speech service handles the audio, and Pingo keeps only the text.
  • Files: only when you choose a backup file to save or restore.

Backups

A backup is a file you create and keep. Pingo does not upload it anywhere. Treat it like any personal file, because it contains your reminder titles.

Ads and payments

The test version shows no ads and takes no payments. Before public release, this page will explain any advertising or purchase data in plain words.

Tester sign-up

If you join the closed test, we use the Google account email you give us only to add you to the Play tester list and to contact you about the test. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Questions or deletion

To remove your tester details, or to ask anything, write to the contact address on the home page. To remove all app data, uninstall Pingo.
