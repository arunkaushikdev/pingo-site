Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Pingo is an Android reminder app made by Arun Datt Kaushik ("I", "me"). This policy explains what Pingo and this website do with your information. In short: your reminders stay on your phone, and I do not run a server that collects them.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Pingo has no account or sign-in. Your reminders, descriptions, priorities, repeat settings, history, streaks and app settings are stored in a database on your phone only. The app works without internet. I cannot see this data, and it is removed when you uninstall Pingo.

2. Permissions and why Pingo asks for them

  • Notifications, exact alarms, display over other apps and full-screen alerts: to ring on time and show the reminder pop-up, including on the lock screen.
  • Vibration, battery optimisation and autostart: so the system does not stop reminders from firing.
  • Speech recognition (microphone): only when you tap the mic to add a reminder by voice. Your phone's speech service handles the audio. Pingo keeps only the text it returns.
  • Files and media: only when you pick an alert sound from your phone, or choose a file to save or restore a backup.
  • Boot completed: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

3. Backups

A backup is a file that you create and store yourself. Pingo does not upload it anywhere. It contains your reminder titles and settings, so keep it as private as any personal file.

4. Ads and purchases

The closed test version shows no ads and takes no payments. The public version may show ads from Google AdMob and offer a paid plan through Google Play. If so, Google may collect device identifiers and usage data under its own policies (policies.google.com/privacy), and where required Pingo will ask for your consent first. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play, and I never see your card details. I will update this policy before these features go live.

5. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers. To learn how many people visit and on what kind of device, it may use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that counts page views, the page you came from, your country, browser, screen size and device type, without cookies, without storing your IP address and without following you across sites (details). Your test-plan ticks are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in its own privacy statement.

6. Closed-test sign-up and the Wall of Thanks

If you volunteer as a tester, I collect the Google account email you give me by joining the tester group, and the name, role and organisation you give in the form. I use the email only to give you access to the Google Play test and to contact you about it. Your name, role, organisation and profile link appear on the Wall of Thanks, and on your certificate, only if you tick the box that allows it, and I will remove them whenever you ask. I do not sell it or share it for marketing. It is deleted when the test ends, or sooner if you ask.

7. Children

Pingo is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all app data by uninstalling Pingo or clearing its storage.
  • Turn off any permission in Android settings at any time. Some reminders may then not ring.
  • Ask me to delete your tester email using the contact below.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date above and, for material changes, say so inside the app.

10. Contact
